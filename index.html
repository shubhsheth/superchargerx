FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you might want to know about working with SuperchargerX.

What does SuperchargerX do?

SuperchargerX is an independent infrastructure company that builds and operates Tesla Supercharger sites across Texas. We identify ideal locations and develop the sites under Tesla's Supercharger Expansion Program — Tesla installs, services, and maintains the chargers, while we deliver fast, reliable EV charging to high-traffic corridors.

Are you affiliated with Tesla?

SuperchargerX is not owned by Tesla. We're an independent company, and we're among the first contracted under Tesla's Supercharger Expansion Program. We use Tesla's industry-leading V4 Supercharger technology to ensure fast, reliable charging for every EV driver.

Why Texas?

Texas leads the country in EV adoption growth but remains underserved by public charging. The state's deregulated grid through ERCOT also allows for 6–9 month grid interconnection — significantly faster than the 18–24 months typical in regulated markets — meaning we can bring new sites online faster.

How can I invest?

If you're an accredited investor interested in learning more about our Supercharger investment opportunities, get in touch using the form below. We'll set up a conversation to walk you through the model in detail.

I own a property in Texas — can you put a site here?

Possibly. We look for high-traffic Texas locations — generally highway-adjacent with 80,000+ daily traffic counts, or urban sites with strong amenities. Reach out via the form below and we'll evaluate your site.
